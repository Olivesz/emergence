Glider adder — watch the ripple carry compute

Pick two numbers, hit Add, and watch a ripple-carry adder resolve bit by bit — LSB on the right, MSB on the left, carry flowing left across the row exactly like a real ripple-carry chain. Each cell is a full adder built from two half-adders: HA₁ takes A and B straight in; HA₂ takes their sum-leg (s₁) and the incoming carry; the two carry legs merge into the carry-out that feeds the next cell.
