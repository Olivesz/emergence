How to read it: exactly one register drives the bus (green) while another latches from it (cyan) each clock. The ring counter steps T1→T6: T1–T3 fetch every instruction (PC→MAR, PC++, RAM→IR); T4–T6 execute it. Nothing here is behavioral — it is 1446 NAND gates and 175 D flip-flops, the two primitives verified in the raw Wireworld rule, wired into one synchronous netlist.
