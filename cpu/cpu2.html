Every gate here is one of two verified Wireworld cells — a NAND and a D flip-flop. The CPU logic is one synchronous netlist of them; the 256-byte store is a memory array spliced in at the clock edge. Write your own assembly on the left and watch it run. See also the simpler SAP-1 panel.
