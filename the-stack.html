Emergence · Act VIII

A working computer, built up from a rule you could write on a napkin.

No magic, no shortcuts — each rung below stands on the one beneath it, and every claim is checked by running the raw automaton. Two universes, one story: Wireworld (built to compute) and Conway's Life (the barest rule there is). This is the ladder from that rule to a processor that runs real programs.

Wireworld  ·  a wire cell becomes an electron iff 1 or 2 of its neighbours are electrons
Conway's Life  ·  a cell lives iff it has exactly 3 live neighbours, or 2 and is already alive
01

The signal

A moving 1. In Wireworld, an electron running one cell per tick down a wire. In Life, a glider — five cells that crawl diagonally forever — emergent from the rule, not built into it. A glider gun is a source that fires one every 30 ticks.

02

The gate verified in the raw rule

Where signals meet, the rule is logic. In Life, gates are literally glider collisions — two gliders annihilate (that's NOT / inhibit), or merge into one only when both are present (that's AND). From them, NAND — the universal gate.

03

Arithmetic 3 + 2 = 5, bit by bit

XOR beside AND is a half-adder; two of those make a full-adder; rippling them adds whole numbers. Flip both inputs to 1 and the sum lanes go dark as the carry lights — binary 1 + 1 = 10 — every bit checked against the bare rule.

▸ "Add a bit" — a half-adder in gliders
04

Routing hardware → a routed gate tick-exact

To wire gates together you must turn a signal and fan it out. The Snark reflects a glider 90°; the duplicator splits one into two — both verified cell-by-cell. And from them, a physically-routed AND gate: input B steered through two reflectors onto the merge, A entering directly, the output escaping on a lane neither input touches — its whole truth table exact and tick-perfect in the raw rule. Real gliders, routed.

▸ Watch the routed AND gate run
05

The computer runs real programs

In Wireworld — the CA built for circuits — the gates compose into a genuine CPU: SAP-1 and the bigger SAP-2 (256 bytes, a stack, ~35 instructions), each one flat netlist of just a NAND gate and a D flip-flop. It runs multiply, divide, GCD, primes, bubble-sort, Fibonacci — watchable live, register by register.

06 · The horizon the honest frontier

A CPU built entirely of physical gliders — routing every wire with reflectors and crossovers, holding state in feedback loops with perfect timing — is a genuine multi-year undertaking (the "Life computer"). The gates and the routing hardware are real and verified; the fully-routed machine is the mountain still being climbed. Nothing here is inflated — the CPU that works today is the Wireworld one; the glider version is honest, real, and reaching upward.

Every gate, adder, reflector, and CPU cell in this ladder is verified by running the cellular automaton itself — source at github.com/Olivesz/emergence. From "3 to be born, 2 to survive" to a machine that computes.
